Rolling cage drone - engineering demonstration (real-time 3D simulation)

RUN
  Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox (double-click is enough).
  Works offline; three.js r128 is bundled in lib/.

CONTROLS
  Space = pause/play    R = restart    H = clean view (hides UI)
  1-7   = camera (Auto, Wide, Chase, Props, Board, Wheel, Ground)
  Speed 0.5x / 1x / 2x buttons in the bottom panel

RECORD AS VIDEO
  Press H for clean view, then screen-record (Win+G, OBS, or QuickTime).
  Full sequence is about 2 min 12 s and loops.

SEQUENCE
  FLY > LAND > ROLL FORWARD > ROLL BACKWARD > STOP > TAKE OFF > FLY,
  then a condensed continuous final shot.

Note: geometry is conceptual. Verify motor torque, rail friction and
gimbal clearances in CAD before building a prototype.
